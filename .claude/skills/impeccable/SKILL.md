# Impeccable: Design Engineering & Craft Skill

## Philosophy & Core Directives

Impeccable approaches every frontend design task with the standards of an award-winning design director:

- **Distill (`/impeccable distill`):** Strip out visual noise, redundant container boxes, and unnecessary decorative badges.
- **Harden (`/impeccable harden`):** Ensure solid contrast, precise 1px borders, robust empty and error states, and strict keyboard accessibility.
- **Quieter (`/impeccable quieter`):** Tone down aggressive saturated colors; keep 90% of the surface in neutral tones and reserve color for meaningful semantic signals.
- **Bolder (`/impeccable bolder`):** Provide confident typography hierarchy, high contrast, and unmistakable primary actions.
